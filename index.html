Streamlined Workflow

How We Turn RAW Footage Into Magic

A frictionless 4-step production pipeline designed to keep you in control while we handle the heavy editing lifted.

01

Footage Upload

Upload your raw video files and assets securely to our high-speed cloud drive alongside your project brief and references.

02

Assembly & Hooking

Our lead editors structure the story arc, cut out pauses, craft high-retention opening hooks, and build initial visual pace.

03

VFX, Sound & AI Magic

We add custom motion graphics, sound effects, 4K color grading, dynamic captions, and AI visual enhancements.

04

Final Review & Export

Review the draft via Frame.io, request timestamped revisions if needed, and receive your final pristine 4K render.
